# Lesson 6: Files and backups

Backups only protect what's in them. A file that lives only on your desktop or your personal Drive is outside the business's backup, and can be lost for good.

**Save work where the business backs it up.** Use the shared storage the business set up, not your desktop, Downloads folder or a personal account. If you don't know where that is, ask.

**Don't keep the only copy.** Anything you'd be upset to lose belongs in the business's storage, not only on your laptop.

**Restoring is the backup's job, saving is yours.** Where Cartman Systems looks after the business's backups, they're tested by actually restoring them. That only helps with files saved in the right place.

## Do this now

- My work files are saved in the business's shared storage.
- Nothing I'd mind losing lives only on my laptop or desktop.

## Quick check

Your laptop won't start and has to be wiped. Which files can be recovered?

- A) Everything, since laptops back themselves up.
- B) Only the files that were saved in the business's backed-up storage.
- C) Only files you emailed to yourself.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Show answer</summary>

**B.** A backup is only as good as what's in it. That's why where you save matters more than how often anyone backs up.

</details>
