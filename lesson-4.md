# Lesson 4: Your devices

Your laptop and phone hold the business's email, files and customer information. Most of keeping them safe happens on its own, as long as it's left switched on.

**Leave the protection on.** If Cartman Systems set up ESET or other protection on your devices, keep it running, and don't pause it to get something to work. If it gets in your way, tell support@cartman.systems.

**Accept updates.** Updates close holes that attackers already know about, and known weaknesses are the ones most often used. Let updates install, and restart when asked.

**Lock the screen.** Use a PIN or password on your phone and laptop, and lock them when you walk away.

**Lost or stolen? Say so at once.** Tell the owner, and Cartman Systems at support@cartman.systems, the same day. A device that held customer information is something the business may have to report, and it can't if it doesn't know.

## Do this now

- Endpoint protection is on and green on my work devices.
- Updates install on my laptop and phone, and I restart when asked.
- My phone and laptop lock with a PIN or password.
- I know to report a lost or stolen device the same day.

## Quick check

Your work phone was stolen last night. It had work email on it. What first?

- A) Tell the owner and Cartman Systems today, so access can be cut off.
- B) Wait a few days in case it turns up.
- C) Change your phone wallpaper on your new phone.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Show answer</summary>

**A.** Signing the stolen phone out of work accounts is quick, but only once someone knows. Every hour it stays signed in is a risk to the business.

</details>
