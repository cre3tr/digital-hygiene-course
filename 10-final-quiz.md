**1. According to the course, what is a primary reason to use an authenticator app for 2FA instead of SMS?**

- A) Authenticator apps are faster to set up
- B) To avoid SIM swapping risks
- C) SMS codes never expire
- D) Authenticator apps work without a phone

<details>
  <summary>Show answer</summary>
  
  <p><b> Answer: B) To avoid SIM swapping risks.</b> The course specifically warns that SMS is vulnerable to SIM swapping attacks, making authenticator apps a more secure choice.</p>
</details>

**2. Lesson 2 suggests using the "Diceware" method to create a strong master password. What does this method involve?**

- A) Adding your birth year to a favourite word
- B) Replacing letters in one word with symbols
- C) Using 6-8 random words
- D) Reusing one strong password with a different ending per site

<details>
  <summary>Show answer</summary>
  
  <p><b> Answer: C) Using 6-8 random words.</b> The lesson recommends this method for creating a master password that is both memorable and highly secure.</p>
</details>

**3. Which of the following is a tool specifically recommended for encrypting sensitive files before storing them in the cloud?**

- A) The cloud provider's share-link settings
- B) VeraCrypt
- C) A zip file with no password
- D) Your browser's saved-passwords list

<details>
  <summary>Show answer</summary>
  
  <p><b> Answer: B) VeraCrypt.</b> VeraCrypt and ESET Secure Data are recommended in the course as tools for encrypting files, adding a crucial layer of security to your cloud storage.</p>
</details>

**4. The "Digital Footprint Cleanup" lesson recommends using a specific service for creating alias phone numbers to protect your real one. What service is it?**

- A) A second SIM card
- B) Google Voice
- C) WhatsApp Business
- D) Your work phone number

<details>
  <summary>Show answer</summary>
  
  <p><b> Answer: B) Google Voice.</b> The course suggests using Google Voice for any public-facing numbers to keep your personal phone number private.</p>
</details>

- A) Check login activity on critical accounts
- B) Install device and app updates
