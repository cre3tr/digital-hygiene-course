# Lesson 5: Customer information

Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 puts duties on any business that holds information about people: customers, patients, members, staff. You don't need to know the law to keep to it. Four habits cover most of it.

**Only what's needed.** Collect and keep only the customer information the job needs.

**Keep it in the business's systems.** Don't forward customer information to your personal email or WhatsApp, or save it to your own phone.

**Keep it out of free AI tools.** Free chatbots may keep what you type. Don't paste in customer names, phone numbers, ID numbers, medical details or anything the business would not publish. Ask the owner which tools are allowed.

**Report anything that goes wrong, at once.** An email sent to the wrong person, a lost file, a device gone. The business has legal duties when information leaks, and they start when someone knows.

## Do this now

- I keep customer information in the business's systems, not my personal email, WhatsApp or phone.
- I don't put customer information into free AI tools.
- I know to report a data mistake straight away, however small.

## Quick check

You need help wording a reply to an upset customer. What can you paste into a free AI chatbot?

- A) The customer's full message, with their name and phone number.
- B) Their file, so the chatbot has the full picture.
- C) The situation in general terms, with no names or details.

<details><summary>Show answer</summary>

**C.** Describe the problem, never the person. Whatever you paste may be kept by the tool's provider.

</details>
