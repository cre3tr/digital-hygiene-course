## Decided

- Free supporting asset, no price, no catalogue line (Ian, 2026-10-23).
- Two public copies from one source: interactive at cartman.systems/hygiene,
  static here (Ian, 2026-10-23).
- Pages here are generated and never hand-edited (`CLAUDE.md`).
- Use is measured from the support inbox, not by link tags (planning notes,
  second round).

## Proposed directions (suggestions, not decisions)

1. **Leave it alone until there is a usage signal (suggestion, recommended).**
   It is built, live and low-maintenance. Revisit when the support inbox shows
   whether anyone finishes it.
2. **Re-check dated facts on a schedule (suggestion).** Phone numbers, support
   lines and tool names go stale. A quarterly re-check of the copy file keeps
   the course honest.
3. **Add a licence (alternative).** A public repo with no licence grants no
   reuse rights. Choose one if sharing is intended, or leave it if not.
4. **A second course, for owners (alternative).** The owner series already
   exists in Cartman's notes; the staff course points to it rather than
   repeating it.

## Questions for Ian

1. **How will you know the course works?** The answer decides whether anything
   else on this page is worth doing. Today the only signal is result emails
   received at the support inbox.
2. Should this repo carry a licence, and which?
3. Who owns the re-check of time-sensitive facts, and how often?
4. Should the static copy ever be retired once the interactive copy has a
   record of use?

## Critique (agent's view, offered as pushback)

- **Two public copies double the maintenance surface for no measured gain.**
  The reason is "more surface area" (decision log, 2026-10-23). With no analytics
  on either copy, nothing says the static copy is read.
- **The course is only as good as its dated facts.** Its value rests on a few
  specifics (who calls from which number, which line to ring). Those are the
  claims most likely to change and the ones a reader trusts most.
- **Free asset, unmeasured conversion.** It exists to open conversations, and
  this repo cannot show how many it has contributed to.
