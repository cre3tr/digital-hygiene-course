## What it is for

A free, six-lesson digital hygiene course for the staff of a small business,
from Cartman Systems [HIGH]. Source: `README.md` and `CLAUDE.md` in this repo.
This repo is the static, read-only copy; the interactive copy is at
<https://cartman.systems/hygiene>.

## Why it exists

Ian decided on 2026-10-23 that it is **a free supporting asset, not a priced
offer** [HIGH]. Source: Cartman's planning notes (`digital-hygiene-course.md`
and the decision log entry dated 2026-10-23). It has two uses:

1. Linked from Cartman's free digital and IT check, as a practical next step.
2. Used in onboarding for new retainer clients, as staff training.

It started as a personal project written for individuals ("Digital Hygiene
Pro"). On 2026-10-23 it was replaced by the staff course (`CLAUDE.md`
"History"; commit `36af0a1`).

## The rules that govern it

- **One source of words.** Both public copies are generated from a single copy
  file, so they cannot drift (`README.md`; decision of 2026-10-23).
- **No price in the course.** Prices live in one planning file only.
- **The static copy sends nothing.** Only the cartman.systems copy can send
  results, and it says so on its start screen (`README.md`; planning notes).
- **Local threats, no invented figures.** The lessons cover Kenyan scams and
  the Data Protection Act without statistics (planning notes, "Draft status").

## Who it serves

onboarding [HIGH, from the planning notes]. Whether it has been used by anyone
outside Cartman's own conversations is `[UNKNOWN]`: no analytics exist on
